Implementation roadmap · version 2.4, approved · 24 Oct 2026

Roamate: from first commit to a phone in your hand

Fifteen milestones in seven phases, plus a TravelPlanner research track. Every design is approved before any code is written. Then come accounts, the model gateway and the city data pipeline, then the solver, and the first working web app (Web Alpha) after M10. A research track on the TravelPlanner benchmark runs next to M11 and M12, real people test the app after M12, and the mobile app reuses the same backend. Work goes on main until Web Alpha, then on branches. Tap any station to see its steps and what you can try when it is done.

Segment length shows rough size: S under a week, M one to two weeks, L two to three weeks, for one developer with AI help. These are guesses, not commitments.

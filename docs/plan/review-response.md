## Summary

All 42 findings were checked against the plan, the step list, the M0.2 draft and `solver_example.py`. The two reproduced solver failures (F24 lunch outside the route, F26 negative budget abort) and the span-cost comment (F29) were confirmed by reading the code against the OR-Tools behavior the review cites. The TravelPlanner rule in F39 was confirmed in the benchmark's README.
